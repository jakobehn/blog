Today I received an email from Microsoft stating that:

*Dear Jakob Ehn, \
Congratulations! We are pleased to present you with the 2012 Microsoft® MVP Award! \
This award is given to exceptional technical community leaders who actively share their high quality, real world expertise with others. \
We appreciate your outstanding contributions in Visual Studio ALM technical communities during the past year.*

This is incredibles news and I really want to thank both the people at Microsoft who nominated me and some of the (now) fellow MVP’s that I have worked with over the last year, both as part of the Visual Studio ALM Rangers program and as part of the TFS Build Extensions community project, in particular [Mike Fourie](http://mikefourie.wordpress.com/) and of course my colleague and main source of inspiration [Terje Sandström](http://geekswithblogs.net/terje/Default.aspx) 🙂

I’m really looking forward to this year, it’s going to be a blast! 🙂
